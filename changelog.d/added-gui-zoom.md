- **Interface zoom.** Scale the whole app, diffs included, from 80% up to 200%
  (capped to what your display fits): press Ctrl/⌘ `=` or `-`, hold Ctrl/⌘ and
  scroll, or use the stepper in Settings → Appearance. Ctrl/⌘ `0` or its Reset
  button puts you back at 100%. Over the file, commit and project lists,
  Ctrl/⌘ and scroll keeps scrolling, so you can extend a Ctrl/⌘-click selection.
