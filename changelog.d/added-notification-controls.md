- **Per-source notification controls.** Every kind of event carries its own pair
  of checkboxes: a row in the activity inbox, a desktop ping, both, or neither.
  Agent sessions, plans, and research join the list, CI checks choose whether
  they watch just your pull requests or every open one, and **Notify on** keeps
  the pings worth having: checks and workflow runs can report every result,
  failures only, or successes only. **Customize…** sets any of it per
  repository, down to muting a whole repo in one click.
