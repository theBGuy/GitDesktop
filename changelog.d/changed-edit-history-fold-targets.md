- Edit history's **Squash** and **Fixup** name the commit they combine into,
  and the menu tells you when no older commit is kept to combine into. The
  commit list also works from the keyboard: arrow keys, Home and End move
  between commits, Enter opens a commit's actions, Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ (Option on
  macOS) move it one place, and Alt+Home / Alt+End send it to the top or
  bottom.
