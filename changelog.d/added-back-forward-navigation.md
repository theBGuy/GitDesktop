- **Go back and forward inside a repository.** Open an issue from a project
  board, follow a `#123` link or jump to a linked pull request, then press
  `Alt`+`←` (`⌘`+`[` on macOS) or your mouse's back button to return to where
  you were; `Alt`+`→` (`⌘`+`]`) or the forward button takes you forward again.
  Every tab you switch to is on the way back too, while moving through a list
  is not. Returning to a board puts you on the same project, view and card.
  Both shortcuts can be rebound in **Settings → Keyboard** and are in the
  command palette, and a shortcut of your own on the same keys keeps working:
  yours wins, and Go back or Go forward stays unbound while yours holds those
  keys.
