- Changes you make while offline are sent to the repository, pull request, or
  issue you made them in once you're back online, even if you've moved on, and
  changes that finish saving after you switch refresh the one you made them in.
  If you switch repositories while one of these waits on a confirmation or a
  check, it stops and says so instead of acting on the new repository: in the
  branch menu, resetting, deleting, publishing, updating, merging, rebasing, or
  switching branches, and discarding or stashing changes; discarding or
  stashing in the Changes list; checking out, reverting, or cherry-picking a
  commit from History, a commit's details, or Compare; in History, undoing the
  last commit, resetting, cherry-picking onto a branch, or deleting a tag;
  checking out a tag; and removing a submodule.
