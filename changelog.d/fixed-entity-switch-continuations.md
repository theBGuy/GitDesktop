- When a release for an existing tag, a promotion, an edit, a delete, a reset,
  or a cherry-pick finishes after you've moved on to another tag, pull request,
  issue, discussion, branch, or commit, the dialog you opened there stays open
  and you stay where you are. A release you're drafting for an existing tag
  keeps its title and notes when you switch away from the app and back.
  Switching repositories closes the New discussion dialog, so every discussion
  starts in the repository you're viewing, with that repository's categories.
  Generate waits until a create or other submission finishes sending, so a
  draft you've already submitted isn't revised by mistake.
